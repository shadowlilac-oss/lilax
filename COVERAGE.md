[`IMPLEMENTATION.md`](IMPLEMENTATION.md). Source paths below are relative to the repository root;
destination paths are relative to `lilax/src/lilax`. Source evidence identifies

cross-provider interoperability remain unestablished. Owner-maintained `*-coverage.md` ledgers
record source symbols, supported restrictions and limitations in greater detail. This ledger is

## Open delivery gaps

- Owner closeouts identify authored callables and source/caller handoffs; no specific missing
  executable unit is established by the former blanket-pending cells. Resource lookup, installed
  dependencies, services and runtime behavior remain execution-unverified, not absent by default.
- Cross-owner model/runtime/trainer, data/checkpoint, Hub/conversion and rollout/serving handoffs
  are authored. Shared checkpoint visibility, state-format interoperability and combined dependency
  compatibility remain unestablished; source `--no-deps` workflows are not compatibility proof.
- Field/variant/combination fidelity is not exhaustively mapped. Source-inherited defects and
  restrictions remain distinct from migration omissions; absence from this table is not exclusion
  or proof of delivery. Owner ledgers retain the more specific source-reading records.
- No-op cloud/dependency stubs, the Qwix compatibility monkeypatch and developer-only regeneration
  machinery are policy-excluded. Docstring-only source pipelines and
  inherited M3 cache limitations do not imply missing source implementations.
- Authored state does not establish execution verification or full parity; no tests, checks,
  imports, builds or installations were run for this reconciliation.
